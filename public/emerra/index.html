BEFORE YOU JOIN

A few things to know.

Can I download Emerra today?

Not yet. Emerra is in development. The plan is to begin with iOS, with Android to follow. Join the waitlist to hear about availability; a release date has not been announced.

Is Emerra only for people who have booked surgery?

No. The planned experience covers three starting points: researching options, preparing for a booked procedure, and recovering after surgery.

Will Emerra provide medical advice?

Emerra is being designed as an informational and organizational companion. It is not intended to diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical care. Always follow your care team’s advice.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

We save your email and interest in Emerra so we can share product updates. No patient account is created, and we do not ask for procedure details, health records, or photographs. Read our website privacy policy for more information.
