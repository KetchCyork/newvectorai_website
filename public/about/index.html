THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Built on experience.
Driven by what’s possible.

Chris York has spent more than 25 years connecting business strategy with enterprise technology. His work spans ERP transformations, acquisitions, and supply-chain change across manufacturing, retail, financial services, and pharmaceuticals.

As CEO of Ketch, he grew an SAP consulting business and expanded into Mexico. At Ameri100, as EVP of Digital and Delivery Excellence, he built the project management and delivery practices that helped teams execute consistently. Today, as Partner and SVP at TSP – The Silicon Partners, he leads SAP delivery and applies AI to the way work gets done.

That experience now meets a new set of tools. Chris works directly with AI agents, Claude Code, and Model Context Protocol, supported by Anthropic certifications. The perspective behind New Vector AI comes from using the technology, building with it, and understanding the organizations and people it must serve.

New Vector AI is an independent venture founded by Chris York.
