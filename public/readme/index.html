NEW VECTOR AI / README

A little context
for what comes next.

New Vector AI is an independent studio creating software and practical education for the AI age. This website introduces the work in progress and gives you a way to follow the launches.

What we’re building

  • Kolloq — a multi-agent workspace that brings different model providers, delegated agents, and an Advisory Council together.
  • Emerra — a companion in development for the plastic surgery journey, from research through recovery.
  • SecureWhisper — corporate-focused voice-to-text software with local speech recognition.
  • AI education — practical content for established and late-career leaders who want to put AI to work.

Launch status

All products are pre-launch. Features described here reflect the current direction and may change before release. The site does not offer product downloads or accept payments.

How the waitlist works

Choose a product or education topic, enter your email, and agree to receive updates. Your signup is saved when the form confirms you’re on the list. Joining does not create a product account or guarantee a release date.

Privacy

Read the website privacy policy to understand what the waitlist collects, who processes it, and how to request removal.

For the site owner

The protected admin panel lets you set the signup-notification recipient, connect email delivery, view entries, and export the waitlist. Signups remain saved before an email service is connected.
